MassDOT: collision engages the entire wall section between expansion joints, γ_CT = 1.0, and φ = 1.0. For stability, earth pressure is dropped (the wall deflects away from the backfill during the impact). For stem design, earth pressure IS applied at γ_p = 1.0 — not because it acts concurrently, but because it has already strained the rebar and the collision strain adds to it.
