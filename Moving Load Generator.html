Load Model Rules
Variable axle spacing

The multi-vehicle case applies only to negative moment between points of contraflexure (unit load on all spans) and to interior support reactions. Headway is the clear distance from the rear axle of the lead vehicle to the front axle of the following one, swept up to twice the longest span. The lane or uniform load is included and scaled with the vehicles.

Impact

I% = 100/S + [40 − 3L²/1600 for L < 80 ft, else 16 + 600/(L − 30)]. L = length of the span containing the section; interior supports and pier sections use the mean of the adjacent spans. Verify against current AREMA Ch. 15 for continuous spans.
