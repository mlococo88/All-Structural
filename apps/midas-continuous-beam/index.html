Civil NX connection

Not checked. Civil NX must be open and show "Connected".

Spans & I-section (web depth varies parabolically)

Web depth D is clear between flanges; total depth = D + ttop + tbot. D varies along each span as a parabola through the left, mid and right values; flange plates are constant within a span. Each element gets a prismatic I-section at the depth of its midpoint (a stepped approximation of the haunch), so more elements give a closer match. Sent to Civil NX as a DB/User "H" section, [H, B1, tw, tf1, B2, tf2, r1, r2] = [total depth, top flange width, web, top flange thickness, bottom flange width, bottom flange thickness, 0, 0], with the element axis at the section centroid. Non-composite steel section only; no deck.

Loads & combinations

DC = γ·A of the I-section (girder only, no deck, haunch or details allowance) is applied as an element uniform load (not as MIDAS self-weight), so MIDAS and the check solver use the same weight. DW and LL are uniform on all spans; there's no pattern loading.
SER-I uses 1.0 on all three.

Material

User-defined (default) sends E, ν and γ directly, so it doesn't depend on Civil NX's database names (density γ = the Loads card value; mass = γ/g). E doesn't affect the moments when there's one material. Defaults: steel E 29,000 ksi, ν 0.30; concrete E = 57,000√4000 psi = 3,605 ksi, ν 0.20. "Civil NX database" needs the exact standard and grade names: "ASTM(S)" is a Civil NX steel standard but "A709-50" is a guess; enter the material once by hand and GET /db/MATL to see the exact names. Switching the type also switches γ to 0.490 (steel) or 0.150 (concrete) kcf.
