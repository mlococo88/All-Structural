MCT Generator & Rating

Live-load distribution factors per girder and dead-load distribution. The Report tab is the calculation report (the old calc sheet is retired).

Prestressed concrete girder design: losses, stresses, flexure, shear, rating, with the calculation report on its Report tab. Formerly psbeam.html.

The Prestressed Bridge Design rebuild, built like the new Steel Bridge Design app (the prestressed engine moved in unchanged).

Steel Bridge Design

Steel Bridge Design (GirderDetail)
Steel girder details: splices, stiffeners, studs, cross-frames, overhang, section, fatigue.
  • Includes: PlateLine sizing and ST-Girder: steel plate girder design check and load rating (Design & Rating view; stgirder.html was merged into it).
  • Sends: every girder's bearing reactions to Substructure Loading.
AASHTO LRFD 10th
Steel Bridge Design (new, in development)
The Steel Bridge Design rebuild (one engine, worked steps for every value).

  • Not for design use yet: use the card above.
