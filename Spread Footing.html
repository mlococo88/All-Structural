Pedestals & Applied Loads
Axes & sign convention (origin at footing centroid, plan view):
• +x points toward the B/2 edge (right); +y points toward the L/2 edge (up in plan); +z is up.
• P positive = downward (compression on soil).
• Vx positive = horizontal force toward +x; Vy positive = toward +y (both applied at pedestal top).
• Mx positive = moment that shifts the soil resultant toward +y; My positive = shifts it toward +x.
• A shear at the pedestal top also creates a base moment V·h (h = pedestal height): +Vx adds to My, +Vy adds to Mx (toggle in Stability). • Load offset ex, ey (per pedestal): where the loads act on the pedestal top, measured from the pedestal centroid, same axes. P of every load type (and the direct factored P) acts there, so +P·ex adds to My and +P·ey adds to Mx for the footing and the pedestal. 0 = centred.
Enter loads at the top of each pedestal. The 3D View and schematics use the same axes (see the labeled x/y/z triad in the 3D scene).
