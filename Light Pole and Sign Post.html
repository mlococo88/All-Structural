Project —
A pole carrying splayed catenary lighting cables plus its own wind. Each cable's dead, wind, ice, and wind-on-ice loads resolve to horizontal and vertical pole reactions; the unbalanced pull drives the IBC §1807.3 embedment and the ACI 318 shaft. Wind is enveloped at the worst direction for each component independently.
Scope (this mode): IBC 2021 · ASCE 7-22 · AISC 360-16 · ACI 318-19 — non-highway light poles and sign supports (site, parking, sports and private signs). AASHTO LRFDLTS highway supports have their own mode (code switch above the Light pole / Sign switch); do not use this mode for them.

Pole properties

tapered tube, 10 segments
Computed pole self-weight (added to dead load)—

Pole discretized into 10 equal segments top→base. Diameter and wall thickness vary linearly; section properties and self-weight evaluated at each segment midpoint. Hollow circular section: A = π/4(D₀²−Dᵢ²), I = π/64(D₀⁴−Dᵢ⁴), elastic S = I/(D₀/2), plastic Z = (D₀³−Dᵢ³)/6, r = √(I/A).

3

Fixture loading

wind, ice & eccentric dead

Each fixture generates a wind drag force F = q_z(h)·EPA·C_f at its mount height (full EPA is exposed regardless of wind azimuth, since EPA already embeds drag and shape). Ice weight uses the accreted volume V = t·(2·A_ice + 4·√EPA·depth) at the global radial ice thickness. The fixture dead + ice weight, acting at the cantilever arm a, produces an eccentric overturning moment W·a directed along the arm azimuth θ — so fixtures at opposing azimuths (0° / 180°) cancel in moment but add in axial and shear.

Pole combined stress

AISC 360 · axial + bending
Governing segment / interaction ratio—

At each segment cut, the shear, moment and axial force from everything above are resolved along the governing wind direction (LRFD). The section is classified per AISC (round → §F8, square/rect → §F7), the nominal flexural strength M_n is found from the plastic modulus, the compressive strength P_n from Chapter E flexural buckling (K = 2.1), and the two are combined with the §H1.1 interaction equation. A prismatic section is governed at its base; a tapered pole is scanned over all ten segments.

Wind is enveloped independently at the worst direction for the pole and for each cable, so component wind magnitudes are summed conservatively rather than shared on one azimuth; cable tension pulls still combine as true plan vectors, so the unbalanced resultant is real. Each pole is assumed to carry half of every cable's vertical load (symmetric span). The IBC §1807.3 solve is the overturning check — no separate tipping factor. Presumptive lateral bearing is paired with service loads; the shaft check uses strength loads. Verify cable tensions, EPA, and ice loads against project criteria; move to a p-y analysis when d/b leaves the rigid range. Preliminary design — sealing requires independent review.
